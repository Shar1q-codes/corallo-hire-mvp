# Demo Script: Single Candidate (10 Minutes)

## 00:00-01:00 Context framing
- "We will evaluate one candidate against one job context."
- "This is uncertainty analysis, not selection workflow."
- Confirm no ranking, no shortlist, no hiring outcome language.

## 01:00-02:00 Load job and candidate context
- Open workspace and job description.
- Open one resume.
- Confirm tenant and evaluation IDs are visible for traceability.

## 02:00-04:00 Intent Alignment walkthrough
- Read the full `intent_hypotheses` artifact item by item.
- For each hypothesis, review evidence, counter-signals, open questions, validation targets.
- State aloud: "If I summarize this, I'm misrepresenting the product."

## 04:00-06:00 Risk Signals walkthrough
- Move to `risk_signals` only after Intent review is recorded.
- Review mechanism -> failure mode format and confidence rationale.
- Reiterate: risks identify what to validate, not who to exclude.

## 06:00-08:00 Interview Guidance walkthrough
- Access `interview_guidance` only after Intent and Risk are reviewed.
- Review objective statements and linked signals.
- Confirm no verdict language and no ranking language appears.

## 08:00-09:00 Show gating behavior
- Attempt to jump directly to later artifact in a fresh evaluation state.
- Show access is blocked until required prior artifact view exists.
- Explain this gate exists to prevent skimming and premature conclusions.

## 09:00-10:00 Close with doctrine line
- "This does not decide. It forces uncertainties to be explicit."
- Log one acknowledgement with explicit validation intent.
- End by pointing to incident reporting and weekly review docs.
