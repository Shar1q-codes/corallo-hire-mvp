# Common Misframings and Hard Refusals

## "Can it shortlist?"
No. HDIS does not shortlist candidates. It only structures uncertainties for one candidate at a time.

## "Can it rank?"
No. HDIS does not rank, score, or compare candidates.

## "Can it tell who to hire?"
No. HDIS does not produce hiring decisions or outcomes.

## "Can it reduce candidates faster?"
No. HDIS is designed for disciplined validation, not candidate reduction workflow.
