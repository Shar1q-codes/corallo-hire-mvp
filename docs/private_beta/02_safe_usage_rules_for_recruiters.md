# Safe Usage Rules for Recruiters

## Non-negotiable operating rules
- Work one candidate at a time for one job context.
- No rankings, no sorting, no batch decisions.
- Risk is a reason to validate, not exclude.
- Do not paste outputs into decision memos as verdicts.
- Use acknowledgements to record what you will validate and what you will not.

## Category-safe interpretation rules
- Treat intent and risk outputs as hypotheses, not facts.
- Use interview guidance to structure validation, not to produce outcomes.
- Keep human reasoning explicit using acknowledgement content, not checkbox behavior.

## Explicit prohibitions
- Do not ask for shortlist/reject/hire recommendations.
- Do not use language such as best, top, score, fit, or recommendation.
- Do not treat artifacts as final conclusions.

## Escalation path
If output appears to drift into forbidden language, report immediately via:
